# SIPC: what First Leaf may and may not say

> Ruling, Sept. 25, 2026. Nothing on the site may say or imply that First Leaf is a SIPC member or that Rosa's holdings are protected. SIPC appears only as a general fact.

## The law

**15 U.S.C. §78jjj(d)(1), "Misrepresentation of SIPC membership or protection"** ([law.cornell.edu](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/78jjj), fetched Sept. 25, 2026, quoted verbatim):

> Any person who falsely represents by any means (including, without limitation, through the Internet or any other medium of mass communication), with actual knowledge of the falsity of the representation and with an intent to deceive or cause injury to another, that such person, or another person, is a member of SIPC or that any person or account is protected or is eligible for protection under this chapter or by SIPC, shall be liable for any damages caused thereby and shall be fined not more than $250,000 or imprisoned for not more than 5 years.

First Leaf is an invented company, so it is not a SIPC member. The site therefore never says "Member SIPC", "SIPC member", "protected by SIPC", "SIPC-protected", or anything that places Rosa's holdings under SIPC. It also never mentions FDIC. Rule G6 and the site crawl enforce this.

## The general fact the site shows

> SIPC protection covers stocks and cash at a member brokerage if the brokerage fails. It doesn't cover crypto, such as Bitcoin or Ethereum. It never covers a drop in price.

Verified against [What SIPC Protects](https://www.sipc.org/for-investors/what-sipc-protects) (fetched Sept. 25, 2026):
- "SIPC protects against the loss of cash and securities – such as stocks and bonds – held by a customer at a financially-troubled SIPC-member brokerage firm. The limit of SIPC protection is $500,000, which includes a $250,000 limit for cash."
- "SIPC does not protect any digital or crypto asset that does not qualify as a "security."" The same page quotes the SEC and CFTC, whose examples of digital commodities include "Bitcoin (BTC)", "Ether (ETH)" and "Solana (SOL)".
- "SIPC does not protect against the decline in value of your securities."

It is used in two places: the FYI account notice "SIPC protection doesn't cover crypto" (shown while an account holds crypto) and the glossary word "SIPC protection".
