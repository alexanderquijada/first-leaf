# "What it is": sources for the ten descriptions

> Ruling 9, Sept. 25, 2026. Each description is checked against the company's or project's own description, kept to plain facts (no praise, no prediction), at about a grade 6 reading level. Company quotes are from each 10-K's "Item 1. Business", downloaded from SEC EDGAR on Sept. 25, 2026. Crypto quotes are from each project's own site.

| Ticker | Description on the site | Source and key words | What changed |
|---|---|---|---|
| AAPL | Apple makes the iPhone, the Mac and the iPad. It also sells services like app downloads and music. | [10-K, Sept. 27, 2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019325000079/aapl-20250927.htm): "designs, manufactures and markets smartphones, personal computers, tablets… and sells a variety of related services"; the App Store and Apple Music | Apple mainly runs a store for other companies' apps, so "sells apps" became "sells services like app downloads" |
| MSFT | Microsoft makes Windows and Office. It also runs a cloud service, where people and businesses pay to use its computers over the internet. | [10-K, June 30, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/789019/000119312526323660/msft-20260630.htm): Windows, Office, Microsoft 365; "Azure is a comprehensive set of cloud services… through our global network of datacenters for computing, networking, storage" | "big" removed (a judgment) |
| NVDA | NVIDIA designs computer chips. People use them for video games and to build AI. | [10-K, Jan. 25, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/nvda-20260125.htm): "GeForce GPUs for gaming and PCs"; "a data center scale AI infrastructure company"; "fabless" (others manufacture its chips) | "They run video games" was loose |
| COST | Costco runs warehouse stores and websites. Shoppers pay a yearly fee to be members. | [10-K, Aug. 31, 2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983225000101/cost-20250831.htm): "We operate membership warehouses and e-commerce sites"; "Our annual fee for these memberships is $65 in the U.S." | "big" removed; "buy in bulk" is not in the 10-K |
| NKE | Nike designs and sells sports shoes, clothes and gear. Other companies make most of it. | [10-K, May 31, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018726000088/nke-20260531.htm): "design, development and worldwide marketing and selling of athletic footwear, apparel, equipment"; "Nearly all of our products are manufactured by independent contractors" | "makes" was wrong |
| AMZN | Amazon runs online and in-person stores. It also rents out computer power over the internet. | [10-K, Dec. 31, 2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1018724/000101872426000004/amzn-20251231.htm): "our online and physical stores"; AWS "on-demand technology services, including compute, storage" | "huge" removed; physical stores added |
| TSLA | Tesla makes electric cars. It also makes batteries that store power for homes and businesses. | [10-K, Dec. 31, 2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1318605/000162828026003952/tsla-20251231.htm): "design, develop, manufacture, sell and lease… fully electric vehicles and energy generation and storage systems"; Powerwall for homes, Megapack for businesses and utilities | "big batteries for homes" was wrong (the big ones are for businesses) |
| BTC | Bitcoin is a digital currency. No company or bank runs it. | [bitcoin.org](https://bitcoin.org/en/): "operate with no central authority or banks"; [FAQ](https://bitcoin.org/en/faq): "a completely digital money" | None |
| ETH | Ethereum is a computer network with its own digital currency, called ether. People also run programs on it. | [ethereum.org](https://ethereum.org/en/what-is-ethereum/): "a decentralized blockchain network… powered by the cryptocurrency ether (ETH)"; "smart contracts" | The network and its currency are named correctly; "small" removed |
| SOL | Solana is a computer network with its own digital currency, called SOL. People also run programs on it. | [solana.com](https://solana.com/learn/what-is-solana): a network; SOL is "Solana's native currency"; "Programs that run on Solana" | Same as Ethereum; the site's own speed claims are left out |

The crypto quotes were read through a summarizing fetch tool, not copied from raw HTML; they match the sites' published wording as far as checked.
