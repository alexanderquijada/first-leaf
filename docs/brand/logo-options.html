First Leaf: three logo options

Each is a seedling with its first two leaves, drawn in simple mid-century shapes, beside the "First Leaf" wordmark in Newsreader. Colors come from the palette: forest, lime and ink. Shown as the lockup, then the mark alone at 64, 32 and 16px. A ships (outlined).

A · Sprout tile (ships)

First Leaf
64
  • A forest tile holds the sprout, so it reads on any background (tile on cream 7.8:1, lime on forest 5.9:1).
  • Two rounded first leaves (a seedling), a cream ground line and a small starburst.
  • Unlike Mint (one leaf in a circle), Acorns (an acorn) or Robinhood (a feather).

B · Open seedling

First Leaf
  • No container: lighter and more hand-drawn.
  • The lime needs its forest outline to reach 3:1 on cream, and at 16px the outline and dots blur together.

C · Starburst sprout

First Leaf
  • The most "mid-century" of the three.
  • Busy at small sizes: the starburst points and the sprout compete at 16px.
